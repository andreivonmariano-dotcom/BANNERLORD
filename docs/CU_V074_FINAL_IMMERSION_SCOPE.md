# CU v0.74.0 final immersion scope and acceptance
24 November 2026. Design scope frozen after Lord feedback. This is the final intended feature boundary for this development pass, not evidence of a finished release. Canonical acceptance PR #99 remains the base. Draft PR #100 is the sole active gameplay-depth candidate. Earlier complete green gameplay-depth build 45377690862 at c23869613cbf4f2fe1bcc99e60acac5b6774da15, artifact 14911064783, is the fallback, not the new immersion code.

## Included
- Seven careers only: Wanderer, Outlaw, Mercenary, Soldier, Knight, Lord, King. Native guided creator and actual starting household; native political status, oath and SaS authority; voluntary career observations and existing progress records. Career duties optional, never mandatory checklist or auto promotion.
- One repeatable personal conversation entry for actual adult living active noncaptive native Heroes. Native Honor, Generosity, Calculating, Valor, Mercy, and current relation determine a shared six-tone voice. Basic opinions open to untracked Heroes; known/related people retain their actual agenda and history. No invented unique persistent personalities or blanket changes to native dialogue. Contextual concerns prioritize real pending petition, siege, active charge, and young children. Private finances/estate counsel require appropriate relationship; only actual family/companion officers, current sworn patron or verified enlisted lord advise the relevant charge. Native action-specific military conversations remain separate.
- Family audience, officer duties, appointment, gifts, petitions and petition follow-up live within the shared conversation hub, not repeated top-level entries. Same existing receipt, cooldown, refusal and native effect guards. Ordinary dialogue remains repeatable without another reward. Supported/refused responses depend on native evidence; no false gratitude for failed transfers. Retain latest petition per person, allow new eligible invitation, not infinite payable stories. One unresolved invitation, existing 70-day/three-request quota remains. No automatic income or relationship edits through the ordinary opinion/concern topic.
- Co-parent conversation selects an actual existing CU-tracked minor child of both native parents and chooses future Kind, Martial, Scholarly, Mercantile, Seafaring or Independent upbringing. Requires reciprocal spouse and nonnegative relation. Same family-growth state, no immediate skill grant, past milestones preserved. Existing automatic milestone behavior still requires BirthAndDeath/native overlap verification. These are modeled conversation choices, not verified autonomous spouse consent.
- Lord/King actual estate visit, garrison readiness or council plans, native action to fulfill and existing weekly review records. Existing estate reports, personal gold and household gifts; no extra treasury, roster change, automated levies or fake court disputes. Charge closes on actual verified evidence even during weekly review cooldown, so player is not told to wait to acknowledge a completed deed.
- Existing witnessed military/battle history, inheritance claims, family and career dossiers retained. Context-aware discussion is not a new autonomous campaign event engine.

## Removed or restricted
- Redundant root-level household/appointment/petition/follow-up dialogue entries reparented to one social branch. Duplicate past-story topic hidden when the actual follow-up is available. No raw database/receipt terminology in new charge descriptions. No irrelevant offers when prerequisite native evidence missing; no unrelated NPC issuing career instructions.
- 20 legacy mutation flags, old creator editor, obsolete MCM UI, artificial companion wage subsidy, duplicate knight battle reward remain held/retired as in prior acceptance audit. Preserve legacy save keys and fallback paths.
- No second personality stat system, global AI simulation, invented family disputes, automatic succession, parallel estate economy or fabricated autonomous actions. Full world-wide evolving ambitions, new disputes with real outcomes, estate expenditure allocation, fully branching native quest library and every older dialogue's personality adaptation remain incomplete; do not advertise them as finished. Keep out of final scope until distinct native evidence justifies them.
