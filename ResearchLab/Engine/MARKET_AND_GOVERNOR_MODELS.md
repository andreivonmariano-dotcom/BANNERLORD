## Market vs construction
- GameComponents.DefaultSettlementEconomyModel.GetTownGoldChange(Town) arithmetic is round((10000 + 12 * prosperity - settlement gold) * 0.25). This is the default model, not a guaranteed full-profile value. ItemConsumptionBehavior.DailyTickTown -> MakeConsumptionInTown -> UpdateTownGold uses active Campaign.Current.Models.SettlementEconomyModel.GetTownGoldChange then SettlementComponent.ChangeGold. Trade access, caravan sell, loot sales, workshop input/output, item consumption, SellGoodsForTradeAction, SellItemsAction, merchant inventory and GiveGoldAction reference same gold. No evidence that paying this market balance directly funds construction. Extra gold can reduce the next positive correction or increase the negative correction; other transactions also affect results.
- Public field Town.BoostBuildingProcess is distinct from inherited SettlementComponent.Gold. Correction to ENG-001/native reference shorthand: Gold is declared on SettlementComponent, inherited via Fief. BuildingsCampaignBehavior.TickCurrentBuildingForTown reads Town.Construction, updates Building.BuildingProgress, reads active GetBoostCost and writes BoostBuildingProcess, checks completion. DefaultBuildingConstructionModel.CalculateDailyConstructionPowerInternal reads reserve, active boost cost/amount, governor, skill/perk helper and loyalty; no guarantee of positive construction.
- ViewModelCollection.GameMenu.TownManagement.TownManagementReserveControlVM.ExecuteConfirm calls Helpers.BuildingHelper.BoostBuildingProcessWithGold(int gold, Town town), whose call/field edges include Hero.MainHero, GiveGoldAction.ApplyBetweenCharacters and reserve field write. Do not assume argument is an incremental payment or call blindly; exact branches and runtime conservation still need proof. Native reserve VM already exists. TownManagementVM.OnProjectSelectionDone calls BuildingHelper.ChangeCurrentBuildingQueue(List<Building>,Town) and ChangeDefaultBuilding(Building,Town); native queue/default selection already exists. Do not recreate.
## Governor evidence
- Helpers.PerkHelper.AddPerkBonusForTown(PerkObject,Town,bool,ref ExplainedNumber) checks nonnull governor, perk ownership, CurrentSettlement not null and equal to target settlement, then applies relevant primary/secondary bonus/increment; returns false when missing conditions. This is one helper, not a universal rule for every governor effect. Helpers.SkillHelper.AddSkillBonusForTown(SkillEffect,Town,ref ExplainedNumber) references governor or owner clan leader role and skill effect; exact branch conditions not yet fully traced.
- DefaultSettlementLoyaltyModel.CalculateLoyaltyChangeInternal includes food stocks, owner culture, policies, projects, issues, security, notable relations, governor perks and drift. It separately checks governor presence and positive loyalty change before HonorLoyaltyGainEffect. Perk path calls HeroicLeader, PhysicianOfPeople, Durable, Discipline, WellStraped; Parade also checks other eligible heroes in settlement. Do not attribute total loyalty change solely to governor. Default construction boost amount calls Relocation and SpringOfGold perks; boost cost checks present governor and GenerosityTownProjectEffect.

## Design decision
Hold L001 (market transfer) as historical compiled-only experiment; no reason to install merely to confirm unrelated payment. F007 records design risk: do not conflate market balance and project reserve. New L002 read-only probe measures native presence, links, market/reserve, project/level/progress and daily model totals via normal native actions. Only a player-relevant choice after evidence: e.g. a family member staying to complete a real project versus accompanying the player, with actual tradeoffs and native authority. Avoid duplicate native governor/reserve menus, no fake construction buff, no competing garrison/recruitment. No claim of useful gameplay until controlled native observations and player critique.
