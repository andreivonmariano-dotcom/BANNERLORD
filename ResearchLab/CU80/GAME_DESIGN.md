# Living Calradia | v0.80 redevelopment design (CU80-GDD-001)
26 November 2026. Proposed next design, not an approved production version. Subordinate to live v2.0 handbook and current v0.74 six-system contracts. No additional runtime module, no eighth career, no save-key rewrite.

## Core experience
Play a person with relationships and responsibilities rather than administer a second kingdom simulation. A native event or current condition makes a real issue relevant; a specific person can discuss it because of their role, kinship, history or situation; player chooses a feasible native action, refusal or defer; actual poststate determines the reply; future conversation and Chronicle recall the confirmed fact. No passive points for opening menus. Native/provider mechanics remain the only source of world outcomes. CU-authored speech is characterization, not independent NPC consent. No global peer simulation.

## Shared surfaces and pacing
One guided creator. Same My Household dossier from Clan/Party, direct personal conversation (Concerns/Family/Standing), existing native Correspondence for current unread/pending local actions, one My Chronicle. Use relevant existing native screen as next step rather than invent management menu. In ordinary play surface one most relevant concern per encounter, optional other topic, always close. Actual safety and availability re-evaluated when a player chooses; do not publish internal evidence strings. Relevance and severity from native state; no universal score or hidden relationship grind. Explicit decline does not mutate world. Defer keeps state unchanged, not a task. Repetition only after materially different observed situation or player request; suppress stale events, don't promise delivery to absent people. Major family/career events outrank routine advice. Initial observation is baseline, never fictional transition.

## S1 — Starting Life
Keep native FaceGen, culture, identity, age, traits, stats and one creator. Each start commits only after valid native contract; actual current station is read back, not inferred from selected label. Progress is native/provider transition and later personal choices, not prescribed ladder. Seven starts:
- Wanderer: independent landless clan; optional one actual companion; immediate loop choose livelihood and actual companion needs, no fief assumption.
- Outlaw: actual landless band, six native low-tier bandits, lieutenant and retained start reserve policy; crime from Fourberie/native only. First meaningful concern is provisions/authority, not fabricated outlaw status.
- Mercenary: valid native employer contract, actual landless party; first discussion of obligations and native pay, no CU salary.
- Soldier: applicant until verified SaS enlistment; first actual host/lord/duty; provider owns rank/wages/discharge. Missing provider = unavailable, not pretend soldier.
- Knight: independent landless sworn house, named patron and 30-day muster, actual squire; choose genuine existing service task or release. No automatic vassalage.
- Lord: actual landed vassal, native owned fief, spouse/child and existing two officers; first conversation about a real local concern.
- King: actual ruling clan and royal seat, spouse/child and actual retainers; native rule and succession, not automatic political perks.
No new creation bonuses. Cancel before irreversible action; partial native creation needs explicit evidence and existing compensation design, not blind replay. Acceptance: seven starts with exact identity/kin/clan/party/fief/provider/resources and reload; invalid provider/target, no duplicate grants. Research SAV-004/005, API-003, AUT-005. Source SandboxStartContract, SandboxNativeStartOptions, existing creator and provider bridge.

## S2 — Family and Dynasty
Lifecycle: native/BirthAndDeath creates, ages, educates, marries and kills. CU observes reciprocal parents/spouses, current residence, actual alive/active/age transitions. Event-specific encounters: birth (acknowledge actual child, visit if present, defer); adulthood (discuss future, offer existing eligible appointment only if adult and local); marriage (congratulate actual reciprocal union, never initiate automatic marriage); separation/reunion (record actual presence, no teleport); bereavement (acknowledge loss, close invalid duties); succession (new player, distinct memory and obligations). Minor children remain read-only. Use existing family dossier and exact pair memory; no daily chores, no education bonus, no auto estate grant. Progress through actual life events, not affection points. Negative cases unrelated child, missing/dead relative, stale observation, repeated load, predecessor memory. Research LIFE-001, SAV-004, AI-004. Source SandboxFamilyCreator/Home, SandboxLifeHistoryState, succession partials.

## S3 — Household and Retainers
Highest priority: actual responsibilities in current situation, not office badges. First slice wounded party and eligible adult same-clan companion/spouse/child with Medicine greater than current effective surgeon; vacant raw surgeon slot, no other roles, native capacity and all existing authority/safety checks. Present appointment through existing native role handler, defer, or leave current arrangement. Read actual roster, raw/effective six roles and provider state immediately before and after. Confirmation is appointment only; subsequent recovery reported as observation, not causality. No extra gold, relation, XP or healing modifier. If incumbent absent but raw slot occupied, don't offer replacement. If no improvement or authority uncertain, no prompt.
Later slices: quartermaster when real supply/capacity concern (native values; no guaranteed savings); scout when relevant travel/spotting context (no simulated path); engineer in safe appointment context for actual siege responsibility (no predicted construction guarantee); governor when owned eligible fort vacant and actor local (reciprocal assignment; no fabricated tax, food or loyalty effect). Governor counsel uses actual current conditions, offers existing settlement controls only. Existing assignments can be reviewed in native UI; no silent overwrites.
Delegation: DUT-003 supports studying actual native issue alternative solutions, with risk/manpower/duration dependent on native issue and provider. Hold new delegation until supported start/finish/cancel, identity, cost, availability and reload contract; do not call issue discovery as read-only or introduce rival recruiting/patrol engine. Improved Garrisons owns its systems. Test occupied, absent, other role, sea/army/battle/siege, SaS uncertainty, stale offer, decline, exceptions, six-slot preservation, reload and real usefulness. Research ROLE-001, DESIGN-001, DUT-003, QST-002, NAV-002, AUT-005.

## S4 — Living Relationships
One exact person + real reason for encounter. Native traits and relationship inform wording, not proof of consent. Family/standing/concerns should share one situation selector rather than independent generic reports. Examples: actual injured party and skilled family member (S3); actual reunion (acknowledge/visit/defer, memory only); financial petition (native actual recipient, quoted cost, explicit choice, verify both gold balances and actual relation; refuse/defer no spend); recognition (witnessed accomplishment, no invented title); audience (hearing not political result). Existing letter inbox informs only relevant actionable local requests, no remote payment or speculative story network. Progress is accumulated verified history and changed circumstances; no universal XP, passive relation or repeating rewards. Distinguish authored motive, observed fact, decision and confirmed receipt. Revalidate exact speaker and target; dedup same event; unknown outcome remains unconfirmed and not retried. Test actor change, repeat, cancel, unaffordable, partial transfer, reload and player critique. Research CON-001, AI-003/004, AUT-005/006, SAV-004. Source existing dialogue, household gift and petition partials; no competing dialogue graph.

## S5 — Career and Sworn Service
Actual native/provider occupation controls authority. Personal Knight oath is CU-owned relationship contract; patron named and alive/available, date and status explicit; muster, escort and scout only from retained verified service path; choices fulfill, defer where allowed, report observed outcome or seek release; no invented battle, automatic fief or custom wage. Knight recognition refers to witnessed task and patron, not native promotion unless actual native action verifies it. Native mercenary/vassal/crown and Diplomacy own politics; SaS owns enlisted status, wages and discharge. Native/Fourberie own crime. Loss of patron/authority invalidates current action, not historical oath. Heir does not inherit personal service. Do not implement custom quest without QST-002 lifecycle tests. Test each career's real action, loss/capture/death, provider absent, expired oath, repeat, reload. Research DUT-003, MIL-001, GEO-001, QST-002. Source existing Knight and SaS bridge.

## S6 — Personal Chronicle
Read-only recent timeline with optional existing surface filters; shared exact person identity and dated verified facts from S1–S5. Separate current status from prior title, founder from successor; native balance sample is not a transaction, actorless callback not player receipt, pre-payment not completion. No fake causality, no manual record-a-career chores, no reward for viewing. Examples: actual completed role appointment; observed family milestone; witnessed service/battle; exact verified gift; unknown financial balance residual stays unknown. Bound history to existing capacities, preserve old serialization and keys; old unverified entries remain historical with provenance, never promoted to verified. Acceptance: same dossier identity, read-only view, repeated load and archive, unrelated events excluded, former player distinct. Research AUT-005, SAV-004/005, ECO-004, VAL-003.

## Vertical slice S3/S4/S6: injured party
Trigger on existing local conversation, not a daily global scan. Situation: positive wounded count, raw surgeon vacant, current effective Medicine known, candidate actual adult local eligible and better Medicine, no unsafe context. NPC line: "Some of our people are wounded. I have experience tending injuries. Shall I look after them?" Display observed count and native skill only when known. Options: assign through retained native appointment, leave current arrangement, defer. Accept must re-read same identity and snapshot; stale = explain and return; one call maximum, verify actual raw and effective role and other raw slots; exception/ambiguous receipt = inspect native party screen, never retry automatically. Confirmed line "I will tend our wounded." Chronicle records appointment, not healing. Later only report independently observed wounded difference under comparable roster and same appointment; no guaranteed effect, no claim all recovery due to healer. Player critique: did this surface a genuine problem at right time, offer distinct options, avoid extra menus, change something useful, remain worthwhile on repeat? If merely shortcut with no perceived value, revise or reject. 
Sequence: pure executable prototype -> native adapter design with existing handler -> safe isolated native assignment and matched recovery control -> player critique -> approved scoped integration -> all relevant regression. F061 blocks native advancement now; restore autosave only with separate explicit authorization and prove isolation first. No production coding/installation/merge/release.
