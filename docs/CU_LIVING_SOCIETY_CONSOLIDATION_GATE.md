Existing six culture name pools, retinue training/gear and modeled inheritance are retained. Dossier now displays the actual native culture ID, descriptive CU conventions, suggested existing upbringing and the established inheritance model; never grants a legal privilege, skill or title. Customs are proposed Calradian gameplay conventions, not authoritative real historical law or personality traits of every individual.

- Sturgia: clan consensus, kin support and household defense; martial upbringing.
- Battania: kindred council, local knowledge and independent upbringing.
- Empire: civil appointment, formal service and scholarly upbringing.

Unknown cultures receive unestablished guidance. Existing editable naming behavior and player-chosen upbringing are preserved.
Future privileges, social customs, marriage traditions, patronage and education should use actual family, property and native office evidence. Existing inheritance score is a modeled claim, not a title or estate grant. No culture automatically determines personality or actual rights.
