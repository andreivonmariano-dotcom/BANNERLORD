## User decision: Q1 → Q2 → Q3, in that order

**Q1, v0.69, highest priority:** resolve Starting Life's native consistency and failure-containment defects before extending the simulation. Native start status, actual parentage, birth rank, spouse/children/siblings, real fiefs, household residences, patron bonds, gifts and native relation scores must agree with the pre-start contract. Check status only after game-owned actions. Stage creation and use safe compensation for partial failures. Check every role's start, and test after save/reload. Do not present source validation, simulation, or a compiler pass as live game acceptance.

**Q2, v0.70+:** design 12 socially credible medieval starts: King, Lord, Knight, Soldier, Admiral, Trader, Outlaw, Beggar, Wanderer, Recruit, HouseholdKnight, NobleCadet. Distinguish birth rank, title, job, clan membership, fiefs, financial means, native patron, service term, real wages, homes, family obligations, legal/political standing and changing social mobility. Keep native Bannerlord status authoritative. A landless cadet does not receive an inherited fief, a household knight is not automatically a landed vassal, and a Recruit is an applicant until real external service.

**Q3, v0.71+:** build persistent NPC identity, stable traits, evolving goals, loyalties, boundaries, verified memories, changing residences and independent actions. A recorded favor or consent requires observed native effect, not an intent label or a relationship threshold. Different people must be able to accept, refuse, negotiate, leave service, improve livelihoods, react to losses and develop personal bonds. Use bounded save data and deterministic policy tests plus live in-game checks.

Do not advance the feature scope ahead of Q1 integrity gates.

## v0.69d | Q1–Q3 audit, native evidence and safe incremental development (21 November 2026)

**New user's directive:** pursue Q1 save-safe partial family recovery, Q2 game-based economy for all twelve roles, Q3 independent verified NPC choices, and audit/review source as each step is implemented. [Draft PR #92](https://github.com/andreivonmariano-dotcom/BANNERLORD/pull/92), branch `fix/v069d-native-evidence-economy-reconciliation`, stacked on PR #91 (not main). [Detailed v0.69d audit and acceptance](V069D_SOURCE_AUDIT_NATIVE_EFFECTS_ECONOMY_AGENCY.md).

**Q1 code:** `SandboxNativeEffectProof` now tests actual native payer and receiver gold balances and native relation score outcomes in character requests and clan mediation. Unbalanced transfers are recorded and uncertain charges cannot automatically repeat. A save-backed `SandboxFamilyRecoveryState` records failed family start, later native counts and manual-review status without changing any real characters on load. A native settlement-menu review is available. Cultural retinue adds and verifies all officers before dismissing an old companion, refuses non-atomic multi-dismissal, and only disables unattached staged Heroes. `SandboxStartContract.RetinueReadyForOtherGrants` and the start flow withhold later gold, troops, renown and items after fief, home or retinue failure.

**Q2 code:** all 12 roles have an observed native source-of-income/obligation/career-evidence declaration in `SandboxRoleEconomyPolicy`. Actual player gold increases and decreases are recorded in `SandboxRoleEconomyState` as unattributed balance changes, saved under `CU_v069d_role_economy`, and visible from the People & Lineage menu. No fake wages, expenses, taxes, household budgets, land income or new native promotions are added. Only the verified Serve as Soldier public API can provide an external daily wage.

**Q3 code:** character-initiated requests now use the live Hero's Honor, Generosity, Calculating, native money, relation, physical presence and persistent goal to decide whether to propose assistance, recognition or no invitation. Accepted gift and relationship consequences are only remembered as factual if they happened natively. Relationship-only `ClanHeadConsents` is explicitly a historical modeled term, **not** native NPC permission. Native estate transfer is blocked until actual game-backed clan-head consent evidence exists. No fake dialogue or self-reported NPC will is claimed.

**Audit priority:** P0 native phase-8 state and already committed spouse/children/parents cannot be fully rolled back. P1 retinue's first native dismissal may still be irreversible, failed-family counts do not prove requested identity, and native transactions should be verified in the actual game. Q2 remains an evidence/role foundation, not complete historical economic simulation. Q3 remains bounded mod-authored initiatives, not full off-screen autonomous AI.
