# Living Society III | Lifetime careers, peer initiatives, household accounts
24 November 2026. Stacked on draft PR #97. Seven careers only. This is a narrow source and design increment, not a playable release. No changes to the guided creator, native economic model, clan or spouse fields, dependency list, launcher or installation.

## Q1 | Lifetime career record
Implemented: CU_LIFETIME_CAREER_1, separate optional campaign save key CU_lifetime_career_1, up to 64 ordered episodes globally. Each episode stores actual Hero ID, a selected seven-path calling, first observation day and last observation day. A new receipt requires explicit player selection and a currently verified condition. Same-day/earlier receipts are rejected. Repeated confirmation of the same role updates the last observation date; a different confirmed role creates a new episode. Dates are observation dates, not a guarantee of continuous tenure or actual promotion date. No automatic inference of retirement, dismissal or role on an unavailable provider. A later character gets separate receipts; no historical episode or resources are copied to heirs. Old CU_CAREER_CHRONICLE_1 is retained separately without assigning its unkeyed historical chapters to a new Hero or inventing start dates. This does not replace existing starting-role chapter/review mechanics or implement native transition actions. It records them after the fact.

Existing native career menu lists eligible record choices and history. Existing recurring evidence checks apply. Soldier requires a valid SaS snapshot and host-party presence. Other roles reject known enlisted or uncertain prior enlisted status. Knight requires a current personal oath owned by this Hero. Other choices reject this Hero's current oath; Knight does not grant vassalage. Mercenary requires actual contract; Lord requires current non-ruling landed clan leadership; King actual rulership. Wanderer requires independence, town presence and no clan settlements. Outlaw needs existing Outlaw calling plus independent band evidence, not an inferred criminal classification. No native promotions, property, legal status, dismissals or enlistments are performed by the menu. A user can record retirement or demotion once actual conditions support another calling. Loss of evidence alone leaves the previous observation intact, not falsely ended.

Next: career episode-linked verified milestone receipts, archival continuity and UI that selects a current chosen career independently of the initial role, explicit native retirement/release and succession events with provider consent, per-episode patron/contract identity, distinct end reason and date only when independently known. Resolve ambiguous simultaneous service; no compulsory progression. Test former role after a release, re-enlistment, lord/crown loss, provider outage and successor with a different Hero ID.

## Q2 | Peer initiatives and native outcomes
Implemented: CU_PEER_INITIATIVE_1, optional CU_peer_initiative_1, bounded 64 modeled peer initiatives. Each record stores actor, target, initiative kind, modeled response and observation day. No player target. Daily observer considers up to 16 known agenda NPCs, every seven campaign days, active living non-captive adults and actual common native party or settlement; at most one new modeled initiative per seven days and no participation by either person within 30 days. Goal selects household cooperation, livelihood contact or patronage interest; native relation and recipient traits feed modeled interest, defer or decline. Existing request and marriage systems unchanged. This is a local model of possible initiative, not NPC speech, actual consent, friendship, marriage or a native relation change. Recent life ledger includes it with explicit provenance. Fixed bounded candidate pool limits coverage and requires later rotation and performance testing.

Future native-effect stages: first observe peer relations and their actual changes without assigning a cause. Then implement consented personal patronage only after verified native action or a supported adapter, no new faction or AI ownership. Native marriage observation remains supported from reciprocal links; courtship and actual marriage require verified game-model eligibility, distinct adult participants, kinship, marital status, native consent/action, reciprocal post-state and no duplicate effect; defer on uncertainty. No direct writes to spouse, clan or native trait fields. Personal conflicts begin as modeled disagreement or witnessed actual relationship changes, no fabricated feud or war. NPC-to-NPC friendship, courtship, marriage, patron service, change of allegiance and conflicts are not enacted by this increment. Native and third-party AI retain authority.

## Q3 | Household finance, property, education, inheritance
Implemented: CU_HOUSEHOLD_ACCOUNTS_1, optional CU_household_accounts_1, 64 personal native Hero gold accounts. Daily read of player, reciprocal living spouse, actual living children with verified parentage and actual living clan companions. Baseline has no delta. Later observation records balance and signed change since last observation, not necessarily a single-day transaction or profit. Same-day/rewound observation rejected; no sums that double-count shared treasury. No fictional revenue, expenses, transfer, tax or automatic inheritance. Person dossier and camp chronicle show live Hero gold, last observed account balance and change, clan name, native clan leadership, clan settlement count labeled as clan property, and existing tracked upbringing milestone (not proof of completed education). Existing family growth and inheritance records preserved. Separate per-Hero account prevents copied predecessor balance on succession; the native game controls actual inherited resources.

Planned economy by calling: Wanderer livelihood and savings; Outlaw native/Fourberie spoils and consequences; Mercenary company contracts and actual payroll; Soldier SaS wages and actual provider status; Knight personal support and expenses without second payroll; Lord actual estate/clan revenues and native property; King royal clan/kingdom finances without parallel treasury or tax engine. Read native evidence first; add explicit categorized receipts only with event provenance. Planned budget: distinguish personal resources, clan assets, obligations, observed costs and voluntary gifts; no mandatory extra payroll or duplication. Personal inventory and clan land are distinct; no invented individual real estate. Existing inheritance claim rules remain descriptive. Native clan leader, actual ownership, succession and consent are required for grants; SandboxInheritanceNegotiationState.NativeHeadConsentObserved remains false until an actual adapter exists. No proposed inheritance is executed. Education uses existing milestone tracker for registered children, future studies and apprenticeships require actual applicable skills or native actions and idempotency. No new education grants or child generation.
