## Original design rule
Decision = verified situation + actor identity/authority + feasible action set + relevant motivation/constraints + player consent when needed + actual outcome. Use a small bounded event-driven personal obligation, not a global AI replacement or nominal ambition. Native campaign AI decides party behavior, native/provider systems own ranks, politics, marriage, garrison, recruitment. Trait interpretation is CU-authored, not a claim to read the NPC's thoughts. Existing engine/model paths for individual motivations, event order, score weights and actual provider compatibility remain unknown. No new automatic relation, gold or AI actions.
