# Original mod inventory: 78 IDs mapped to workstreams

**Historical source:** the user's `modlist` Google Drive folder, containing `Modules.rar.001` through `Modules.rar.012`, and the extracted inventories committed at [Data/Migration_78.csv](../Data/Migration_78.csv) and [Data/CalradiaUnified_78_Mod_Acceptance_Checklist.csv](../Data/CalradiaUnified_78_Mod_Acceptance_Checklist.csv).

Each historical mod appears **once** below under its primary development milestone. This is classification for future original implementations. It is **not a claim of porting, compatibility or ownership**. Exact archive version and rights notes remain in the CSV source. War Sails integration (M8) adds cross-cutting tests for affected features; it does not double-count historical IDs.
