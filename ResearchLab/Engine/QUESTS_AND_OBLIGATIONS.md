## Design hypothesis, not implementation
One bounded household obligation tied to existing ENG-002: a governor with relevant construction/loyalty contribution and a real active construction project wants to remain in post until a specific milestone. Player may agree, appoint another eligible governor through native management, or decline/bring them along. Track actual actor/holding/project identity, progress/level, role/presence, authority and native event. Native construction and existing UI remain authoritative. If project completed while agreement conditions held, close once and remember only verified facts; if owner/actor/project changes, cancel or reassess, not fabricate success. No free relation reward, artificial delays or duplicate funding/control menu. Avoid an obligation if it is just a report or chore. A quest journal entry is conditional on verified native quest integration, not required for every interaction. For native issues, prefer existing quest routes over duplicate offers. Compare repeated usefulness and cost with native baseline; discard if no new meaningful decision.
