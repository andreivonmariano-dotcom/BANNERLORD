# Calradia Unified | Seven careers and living society foundation
Decision: 24 November 2026. Seven top-level careers are final. This design extends the existing V070 seven-path contract; it does not change the selector, native start contracts, family rules, or release gates. No eighth career. The six historic role IDs remain readable without converting existing campaigns.

## Q1: complete career design
The three existing chronicle chapters are retained as early milestones. The following longer loops are design targets, not claims of implementation. Career is a calling, not an automatic grant of political rank.

| Career | Repeatable loop and responsibilities | Rights and progression evidence | Failure and recovery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wanderer | Travel, trade, craft, work, support household, seek patrons | Freedom of independent travel under native rules; three distinct towns are existing chapters; later livelihood, reputation, household security and sponsorship | Insolvency, captivity, loss of livelihood; lawful work, family assistance, voluntary service |
| Outlaw | Maintain band, supplies, loyalty and hiding places; evade or negotiate native consequences | Control only actual party; existing chapters verify party size and survival; reputation and reconciliation follow native/Fourberie evidence | Band desertion, arrest, loss of patron; restitution, amnesty or lawful service where native systems support it |
| Mercenary | Negotiate actual contract, muster company, serve, maintain payroll and provisions, renew or depart | Native mercenary contract, documented pay and service; existing seven-day and further 21-day chapters; reputation and patron offers | Contract loss, losses, debts, discharge; rehire or independent retirement |
| Soldier | Join named lord through SaS, train, obey duties, campaign, take leave, return or discharge | SaS owns actual Recruit/Sergeant/Captain grades and pay; existing enlisted, seven-day and thirty-day chapters | Wounds, capture, discipline, patron death, provider unavailable; record uncertainty without fake discharge or pay |
| Knight | Sworn-house oath or verified younger-sibling origin; 30-day muster, final seven-day window, escort, petitions, patron service | Actual personal oath, confirmed muster and two field escorts; witnessed merit supports requests for recognition, not automatic land or vassalage | Missed muster, captivity, broken oath, patron death; release or consensual renewal |
| Lord | Manage real noble house, estates, petitions, retainers and family duties; native vassal obligations | Native clan leadership, ownership, actual appointments; existing first fief, seven-day tenure and second settlement chapters | Estate loss, family dispute, loss of title; retain historical deeds, seek lawful restitution or alternate service |
| King | Rule actual kingdom, convene council, protect subjects, oversee royal family and succession claims | Native ruling clan and leader, actual office, existing ruler confirmation, first council and third council chapters | Abdication, lost crown, captivity, death; native succession, historical royal identity preserved |

No mandatory ladder. Retirement, demotion, change of employer, loss of property and reconciliation are valid. No forced conquest. New career transitions require eligibility, consent, native/provider confirmation, unique event receipt and persistence. An unconfirmed request never changes the recorded authority. Existing chronicle currently stores one starting role and stops on a role mismatch. Next implementation must preserve its existing CU_CAREER_CHRONICLE_1 data and maintain separate career episodes with start/end evidence, not overwrite the first record. Before adding an episode, check idempotency, duplicate day/event handling and migration in a new save version. Native service snapshots and actual native office remain controlling.

## Q2: shared NPC agency and memory
Reuse SandboxCharacterAgendaState, SandboxAgencyDecisionState, SandboxSocialBondState, SandboxLifeHistoryState, SandboxPetitionStoryState, and household/service/succession observers. No parallel native hero simulator or duplicate relationship field. Native Hero ID is identity. Keep existing native honor, valor, mercy, generosity and calculating traits; do not replace them or claim inferred motives are observed facts.

Design:
- Stable identity: hero ID, culture, actual kinship, actual clan and age. Career intent and modeled ambitions are separate from current native office.
- Needs: household security, livelihood, safety, recognition, duty, affection, independence. Each is modeled from current circumstances; display as an inference, not a native trait or disclosed thought.
- Ambition selection: legal eligible goal, relevant native traits, wealth and relationship, active duties, confirmed previous outcomes. Reevaluate after real changes and on bounded daily work; use stable tie breaks and cooldowns. Choose to act, defer, or decline. Never schedule autonomous movement or diplomacy.
- Memories: witnessed event kind, campaign day, actor/target IDs, source, outcome, stable event key. Separate observed fact, CU-authored interaction and inference. Birth, death, marriage, inheritance claims, service, gifts, refusals, military receipts and appointments can feed the same journal. Native action completion is not inferred from an offer or dialog click. Keep irreversible genealogy separate from bounded recent memories.
- Relations: native relation is authoritative; CU's history describes support/refusal, observed deltas, personal obligations and past service. No invented loyalty meter. Prior confirmed history may affect modeled initiative without changing relation itself.
- Scheduler: initially existing co-located, living active adult heroes only; no captive interactions, no duplicated requests, one pending invitation, existing 70-day cooldown and three-request lifetime cap. A declined or expired request does not execute an action. NPC-to-NPC autonomous proposals and wider populations require separate budgeted implementation and live acceptance.
- Persistence: reuse current CU_SOCIAL_BONDS_1 and agenda save records. Version any new schema, migrate read-only, fail closed on malformed data, avoid name-based identity, bound event count, keep chronology and avoid duplicate receipts. Test save/quit/reload, missing heroes, deaths, captivity, role changes and long campaign loads.

First increment: existing persisted social bond history now feeds CanReapproach. Two or more refusals outnumbering support plus recorded negative relation deltas and current relation <= -10 suppress a modeled request. One refusal does not suppress it; current relation recovery or balanced support can reopen eligibility, subject to existing quotas. No new native side effects. Offer and resolution now require an active, non-captive adult. UI distinguishes modeled ambition from observed fact.

## Q3: hierarchy as independent dimensions
- Birth status: verified parentage, birth order, cultural house membership; noble cadet is not an eighth career. Genealogy grants no land or office.
- Career: seven-path calling and historical episodes, separate from occupation. Trader/Beggar are Wanderer pursuits or conditions, Recruit a Soldier grade, HouseholdKnight a Knight subtype, Admiral a verified naval office.
- Occupation and office: squire, steward, factor, governor, officer, captain, marshal or naval command require their own native/recorded authority. A surname or title string is not proof.
- Personal allegiance: named patron, actual service contract or sworn oath with independent status, dates, responsibilities and release. A personal oath is not native vassalage.
- Political authority: native clan membership/leader, kingdom, mercenary status and ruling clan. Display independent clan, native mercenary clan, native kingdom clan member/leader, ruling-clan member or native ruler only from actual state. Contradictory evidence stays unverified.
- Property and residence: actual clan settlement count and independently observed home. A claim does not grant property; a resident is not necessarily owner.
- Rights and duties: use career contract as guidance, actual native and SaS authority for actions; no duplicate payroll, diplomacy, crime, garrison or combat. Native political leadership, marriage, birth/death, property and succession retain authority. Culture-specific claim rules remain descriptive.
- Social mobility: sponsorship, contract, enlistment, oath, appointment, landed status or native succession; conditions and eligibility differ. No artificial rank from gold, age, skill, renown or kinship alone. Defeat or loss of office changes current standing, not past history.

First increment: career chronicle review displays role-specific duties, current native political standing, native clan settlement count and recorded oath/service status separately. It explicitly labels the chronicle career as recorded, not a verified current office. Existing career episodes and save keys are not changed.
