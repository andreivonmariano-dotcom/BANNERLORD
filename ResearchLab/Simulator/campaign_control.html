Calradia Campaign Laboratory

Actual imported D001 baseline. All future events are hypothetical. No native game, map movement, combat or save modification.

Settlement scenario

Shocks are explicit user inputs. Market gold is not clan or construction money.

NPC decisions

Each active nonplayer clan leader chooses at most one action daily. Guard funding requires a stationary friendly party; it is a security intervention, not a troop mission. Family aid uses leader funds. Truces are experimental overlays between eligible factions, not native diplomacy.

Accounting

Market correction is an external modeled flow. Watch and overture expenses are recorded sinks; gifts are transfers. Missing taxes, wages, trade and other native processes are not modeled.

Model boundaries

Daily security drift and prosperity pressure, town housing and market correction use selected native-derived arithmetic. NPC scoring, funding, relation increments and truce acceptance are authored research policies.

Party positions, personnel, family eligibility and native stances stay at their imported values except the experimental truce and relation overlays. No births, marriages, death, conquest, independent movement or battle resolution.
